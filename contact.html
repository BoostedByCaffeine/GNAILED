Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I know items are authentic?

Every item undergoes rigorous multi-point authentication by our expert team. We provide a certificate of authenticity with each purchase and offer a money-back guarantee if authenticity is ever questioned.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day return policy for items that don't match their description. Items must be returned in the same condition as received. Buyer pays return shipping unless the item was misrepresented.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 24-48 hours. Domestic shipping typically takes 3-5 business days. International shipping varies by destination but usually arrives within 7-14 business days.

Can I sell my items to Gnailed?

Absolutely! We accept luxury items in good condition. Submit photos and details through our contact form, and our team will review and provide a quote within 48 hours.

Do you offer authentication services?

Yes! We offer standalone authentication services for items you already own. Contact us with photos and details, and we'll provide a detailed authentication report with a certificate.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. All transactions are secured with industry-standard encryption and buyer protection.
