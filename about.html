About Gnailed

Where luxury meets sustainability. Every piece tells a story.

Our Story

Gnailed was born from a simple belief: luxury fashion deserves a second life. In a world of fast fashion and disposable trends, we saw an opportunity to create something different — a marketplace where authenticated designer pieces find new homes and new stories.

Founded by fashion enthusiasts who understand the value of quality craftsmanship, Gnailed bridges the gap between luxury and accessibility. We believe that pre-loved doesn't mean pre-loved less. Every Chrome Hearts tee, every AMI Paris sweater, every Ralph Lauren classic carries its own unique character.

What We Stand For

Our core values drive everything we do

100% Authenticated

Every item that comes through Gnailed undergoes rigorous multi-point authentication. Our team of experts examines stitching, materials, tags, and provenance to ensure you're getting the real deal.

  • Expert verification by trained authenticators
  • Multi-point inspection process
  • Certificate of authenticity with every purchase
  • Money-back guarantee if authenticity is questioned

Circular Fashion

The fashion industry is one of the world's largest polluters. By choosing pre-loved luxury, you're making a statement that style doesn't have to come at the planet's expense.

  • Reduce waste by giving garments a second life
  • Lower carbon footprint compared to new production
  • Eco-friendly packaging materials
  • Partner with environmental initiatives

Built on Trust

Gnailed is more than a marketplace — it's a community of fashion lovers who appreciate quality, authenticity, and sustainability. We connect buyers and sellers in a safe, transparent environment.

  • Secure payment processing
  • Buyer and seller protection programs
  • Transparent pricing and condition reports
  • Responsive customer support team

Condition Matters

We don't just authenticate — we grade. Every item receives a detailed condition assessment with clear photos, measurements, and honest descriptions of any wear or imperfections.

  • Detailed condition grading system
  • High-resolution photography from multiple angles
  • Precise measurements for perfect fit
  • Transparent disclosure of any flaws
